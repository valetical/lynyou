about me

i've been making music since around 2017 but didn't take it "seriously" until 2020 or so. you can find info about some of my old / inactive aliases in the other projects page.

my earliest music (2017-2019) mostly consisted of "meme rap" or dumb songs i would make with my friends. in 2020, i started releasing music as c4pri and wanted to create more "serious" songs. late 2021 is when i decided to actually try to improve at producing.

i don't think i've ever really had a specific plan for what i wanted to do with music which probably explains all the different aliases and side projects i've started. i've been lucky enough to have people stick around through all of that and i'm super grateful. i'm still learning more about myself all the time so i don't think i'll ever be fully satisfied or done figuring out what i want my music to sound like

other interests

outside of music, i have always had a love for Valve games, specifically the Half-Life, Portal, and Left 4 Dead franchises. i enjoy looking at early internet culture or old YouTube videos, and if it wasn't obvious enough by the website's theme i like UNDERTALE / deltarune. i also like Super Mario 64 and more specifically Super Mario 64 speedrunning. i've always been interested in old tech, old software, older versions of Windows (i like Longhorn), random shit like that

regrettably i didn't get into much of anything else during my childhood and early teen years because of my obsession with competitive games and my unhealthy attachment to having FL Studio open all the time, so most of what i just mentioned is stuff that i liked growing up that i still like

if you have been familiar with me for a while you would know i have struggled with sticking to one identity throughout the years so i wanted a way to document my stuff and to keep everything in one place. if you're curious about anything else, just dm me lol
